The Data in Motion team is pleased to announce the release of Cloudera Flow Management 2.1.7 Service Pack 5, based on Apache NiFi 1.28.1 and supporting Cloudera on-premises 7.3.2. This service pack improves stability and security by addressing bugs and upgrading dependencies, while also providing the NiFi 2 Pre-upgrade check to prepare for future migrations.

## Release Highlights

1. **Support for Cloudera 7.3.2:** This Service Pack is purpose-built and tested to run on Cloudera 7.3.2, allowing customers to smoothly transition to upgrading the platform before migrating from Cloudera Flow Management 2.1.7 to Cloudera Flow Management 4.x. End of Support (EoS) for this release remains September 2027 in line with all other Cloudera Flow Management 2.1.7 Service Pack releases, so migration to Cloudera Flow Management 4.x is encouraged at the customer’s earliest convenience after upgrade.
2. **Based on Apache NiFi 1.28.1:** Cloudera Flow Management 2.1.7 Service Pack 5 is based on the final Apache NiFi 1.x release (1.28.1), ensuring customers receive the newest features, upgrades, and patches to minimize vulnerabilities and cover essential CVEs for compliance.
3. **Bug fixes, security patches, and performance improvements:** This release includes general bug fixes, security patches, and performance improvements to enhance the overall reliability of the platform.
