The latest Hive JDBC Driver (version 2.6.36) is now live from our downloads page. This release updates several third-party libraries to ensure stability and security compliance for your data connectivity needs.

## Release Highlights

1. **Updated third-party libraries:** The connector now uses the following third-party libraries:
- httpcomponents-httpcore5 5.4.3 (previously 5.4.2)
- log4j-api 2.25.5 (previously 2.25.4)
- log4j-core 2.25.5 (previously 2.25.4)
- log4j-slf4j-impl 2.25.5 (previously 2.25.4)
- netty-transport-native-epoll 4.2.15.Final (previously 4.1.130.Final)
- netty-transport-classes-epoll 4.2.15.Final (previously 4.1.130.Final)
